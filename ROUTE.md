## What to do at each milestone

### Clear Story Chapter 1

Unlocks: Draw. Requires: nothing. Confidence: official.

1. Open Draw and set your Custom list: 3 SSR hunters you would really use. You can change it any time.
2. Keep Essence Stones for Rate Up banners (see the Draws tab).

### Clear Normal Chapter 2

Unlocks: Gates and the Shop. Requires: Clear Story Chapter 1. Confidence: official.

1. Run Gates every day. They give Sung Jinwoo EXP, the most important daily reward, plus Gold and growth materials.
2. Tap the Reward Settings icon on a Gate to pick the material you need. Shadow Energy is the quickest Total Power.
3. After clearing, send mining teams from Go to Gate Mining. Cleared gates leave the mining list after 168 hours.

> Claim Gate mission rewards before Jinwoo levels up. A level-up resets the missions, and unclaimed lower-tier rewards may be lost.

### Clear Normal Chapter 3

Unlocks: Gate sweeping. Requires: Clear Normal Chapter 2. Confidence: official.

1. Sweep Gates you have cleared; each sweep uses a Sweep Point.
2. Sweeps count only the Final Boss for Special Commissions. Play the Gate yourself when you need kill counts.

### Sung Jinwoo reaches level 7

Unlocks: Craft. Requires: Clear Story Chapter 1. Confidence: official.

1. Crafting never fails. Craft hunter materials and weapons as soon as you have the parts.

### Clear Chapter 4, Lizards

Unlocks: Fuse and Change Rune. Requires: Clear Normal Chapter 3. Confidence: official.

1. Put a Skill Rune on each of Jinwoo's 3 skills; a rune turns a skill into a stronger Rune Skill with its own element.
2. Fuse spare runes and Blessing Stones. Each fusion adds Fusion Points, and a full gauge guarantees a higher-grade result.

### Join an active guild

Unlocks: Guild Boss and the Guild shop. Requires: Clear Normal Chapter 2. Confidence: guide.

1. Pick a guild that clears the Guild Boss every season. The Guild shop sells Weapon Custom Draw Tickets.
2. Guild Boss uses 6 hunters: Jinwoo plus 3 in Team A and 3 in Team B. Both teams' best scores add up.

> New members cannot fight a Guild Boss season already running, so join early.

### Clear Chapter 5, Episode 1: The Hidden Quest

Unlocks: Artifacts. Requires: Clear Chapter 4, Lizards. Confidence: official.

1. Equip pieces whose main stat matches the hunter's recommended stats, with at least 2 matching sub stats.
2. Enhance to +5, +10, +15 and +20; each adds or raises a sub stat. A failed attempt adds 1% to the next one's bonus rate.

### Clear Chapter 5

Unlocks: Unstable Dungeon (artifact farm). Requires: Clear Chapter 5, Episode 1: The Hidden Quest. Confidence: official.

1. Set the artifact you want in Reward Settings, then clear with your free keys every day. Keys are only spent on a clear.
2. Clear a tier's Battle Missions to unlock sweeping it.
3. Finish the weekly Conquest Missions there; they give artifact materials.

### Clear Hidden Chapter 1, Episode 8: Unknown Power

Unlocks: Artifact Crafting. Requires: Clear Chapter 5. Confidence: official.

1. When farming will not give the main stat you need, craft the piece: you choose 1 main and up to 2 sub stats with extra materials.

### Clear Chapter 7-4

Unlocks: Battlefield of Trials and Special Commissions. Requires: Clear Chapter 5. Confidence: official.

1. Climb Battlefield of Trials in Player and Hunter mode: Rate Up Draw Tickets, Essence Stones and Gold. Every floor is weak to all elements.
2. Special Commissions pay a one-time reward per kill-count tier on listed monsters.

### Clear Chapter 7-6

Unlocks: Hard story. Requires: Clear Chapter 7-4. Confidence: official.

1. First clears on Hard give Essence Stones and Custom Draw Tickets. Push Hard whenever your power allows.

### Clear Chapter 8

Unlocks: Battle Class. Requires: Clear Chapter 7-6. Confidence: official.

1. Claim the Battle Class reward each time your best Sung Jinwoo Mode team passes a Total Power threshold: Essence Stones and Gold.
2. Armory bonuses count towards it.

### Clear Chapter 9

Unlocks: Army of Shadows; necklace and bracelet slots. Requires: Clear Chapter 8. Confidence: official.

1. Level the Army of Shadows with Shadow Energy; it raises every Shadow and is one of the biggest Total Power gains.
2. Put your strongest Shadow first in the team; the leader's power applies to the whole team.
3. Fill the new necklace and bracelet slots.

### Clear Chapter 10

Unlocks: Battlefield of Time; Edge of Illusion and Dreams. Requires: Clear Chapter 9. Confidence: official.

1. Battlefield of Time: clear each boss within 1:30 for every top Achievement Reward. Seasons run 5 weeks (4 to play).
2. Edge of Illusion and Dreams: co-op bosses with 5 free tickets a week during a season.

> Edge of Illusion tickets are not given off-season, so use each week's tickets.

### Clear Chapter 11

Unlocks: Successors. Requires: Clear Chapter 10. Confidence: official.

1. Successors follow Jinwoo's level. Raise one through its exclusive weapon, artifacts, skills and upgrades. Legacy Seals draw more.

### Clear Chapter 12

Unlocks: Ring and earring slots. Requires: Clear Chapter 11. Confidence: official.

1. Earrings have one main stat (Additional MP), so crafting one needs no main-stat material.

### Sung Jinwoo reaches level 30

Unlocks: Armory. Requires: Sung Jinwoo reaches level 7. Confidence: official.

1. Every Jinwoo weapon you own goes into the Armory and adds part of its Total Power.
2. Enhance all slots evenly with Marks of Time.

### A hunter reaches level 40

Unlocks: Hunter Upgrade (15 per hunter). Requires: Sung Jinwoo reaches level 30. Confidence: official.

1. Upgrade your main team with Mana Power Elixir from Gates. Hunters cannot pass Jinwoo's level, so his level comes first.

### Clear Chapter 14

Unlocks: Power of Calamity; Challenge Medals. Requires: Clear Chapter 12. Confidence: official.

1. Power of Calamity: two teams, best damage counts. Ranking starts on Hard.
2. Claim last week's rank rewards during the next week; after that they are gone.

> Play is blocked on Wednesdays 23:50 to 00:00 UTC while the ranking is calculated.

### Clear Chapter 15

Unlocks: Battlefield of Chaos, Gems, Reverse Mode. Requires: Clear Chapter 14. Confidence: official.

1. Battlefield of Chaos gives gems. Clear a tier's Battle Missions to sweep it; Tier 16 and up gives gem promotion material.
2. Gem stats by colour: Attack%, HP%, Crit Damage, Precision, MP Consumption Reduction, Defense%.
3. Reverse Mode replays chapters with hunters for its own Battle Missions.

### Clear Normal Chapter 18

Unlocks: Mind Rift (core farm). Requires: Clear Chapter 15. Confidence: official.

1. Farm Cores here. Clear-count rewards follow your Battle Tier.

### Clear Hard Chapter 10

Unlocks: Cores. Requires: Clear Chapter 7-6, Clear Chapter 10. Confidence: official.

1. Equip Cores that match Jinwoo's element first, then the recommended stats shown at the top of each slot (Mind, Body, Energy).
2. Level, enhance and limit-break the ones you keep. Feeding a limit-broken core passes on its tier.

### Register a Legendary Skill Rune or Blessing Stone

Unlocks: Skill Research. Requires: Clear Chapter 4, Lizards. Confidence: official.

1. Later (4 million Total Power), synthesise Transcendent runes and stones here. Materials come from the Honor Tokens shop and Legendary fusion or refinement.

### Fill gem page 1 with Rank 4+ gems

Unlocks: Gem page 2. Requires: Clear Chapter 15. Confidence: official.

1. Refine Rank 6 gems of B tier or lower into A tier with the same stats. It costs Essence Stones; a full point gauge guarantees your goal.

### Job Change: Night Lord

Unlocks: Second weapon slot for Jinwoo. Requires: Clear Chapter 9. Confidence: official.

1. Job Change tests open from story missions and achievements; tap the Job Emblem on the Sung Jinwoo screen to see yours.
2. Job Changes also give Stat Points, new skills and Blessing Stones.

### Clear Normal Chapter 23

Unlocks: Architect's Test, Overseer's Forbidden Library, Hunter Co-Op Training. Requires: Clear Normal Chapter 18. Confidence: official.

1. Architect's Test (seasonal) gives Essence Stones; its level follows your Battle Tier.
2. Overseer's Forbidden Library (seasonal): every hunter counts as every element; stage rewards once per season.
3. Hunter Co-Op Training: train one element's classes first.

### Main team reaches 2 million Total Power

Unlocks: Intermediate farming. Requires: Clear Chapter 8. Confidence: official.

1. Spend Honor Tokens on Legacy Seal, Hunter Training Handbooks and the Transcendent Stone Selection Chest.
2. Clear Edge of Illusion and Dreams, Battlefield of Time, the Overseer's Library and the Architect's Test every season.

### Clear Normal Chapter 25

Unlocks: Workshop of Brilliant Light. Requires: Clear Normal Chapter 23. Confidence: official.

1. Your first entry is free; after that each entry costs an invitation, and 2 free invitations come back each daily reset.
2. Seasons last 2 weeks and the recommended element changes by tier. Clearing a difficulty lets you sweep it and every lower one.
3. Shards of the Monarchs from here level Remnant Power.

### Clear Workshop of Brilliant Light Tier 1

Unlocks: Remnant Power. Requires: Clear Normal Chapter 25. Confidence: official.

1. Summon a Monarch in battle for buffs. Each Remnant level gives Passive Points for its skills.

### Job Change: Monarch of Shadows

Unlocks: Shadow Enforcer. Requires: Job Change: Night Lord. Confidence: official.

1. Send Shadows on Enforcer missions every day; more Shadows allow longer missions.
2. Official picks: Abyssal Fragment, Shadow Energy and Shadow Skill Scroll I for Total Power; Marks of Time I for the Armory.

### Clear Hard Chapter 25

Unlocks: Expert story and Figures. Requires: Clear Hard Chapter 10. Confidence: official.

1. Expert bosses unlock Figures, random stats for Jinwoo and hunters. Lock a good stat with Seals of Unity, re-roll the rest with Polish of Change.

### Main team reaches 4 million Total Power

Unlocks: Advanced challenge content. Requires: Main team reaches 2 million Total Power. Confidence: official.

1. Transcendent runes and stones are now essential (Skill Research). Try Amplification on them.
2. Push Workshop of Brilliant Light, Simulation Gate, Power of Calamity and the Deep Containment Zone.

### Clear Expert Chapter 8

Unlocks: Deep Containment Zone. Requires: Clear Hard Chapter 25. Confidence: official.

1. Four hunters of one element activate Union, a once-per-battle burst.
2. Keys refill weekly. Multiplay spends extra keys for more rewards in one run.

### Clear Workshop Tier 10 in Sung Jinwoo Mode

Unlocks: Hall of the Monarchs. Requires: Clear Workshop of Brilliant Light Tier 1. Confidence: official.

1. No entry cost and unlimited retries. Your season score is the sum of all 3 floors.

### Reach Battle Class 85

Unlocks: The First Land (high-tier Workshop). Requires: Clear Workshop of Brilliant Light Tier 1. Confidence: guide.

1. A June 2026 community guide reports The First Land opening here. It is not in the official guide yet; check in game.
